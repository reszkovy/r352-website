02

How we work — the r3loop.

r3loop is our 8-step operating methodology. Every engagement runs through this loop, whether it is a 5-day Diagnostic or a 12-month Operating Partnership. Depth of each step adjusts to engagement size, but the sequence stays constant. This is what gives our work its predictability.

01
Diagnose
Where does delivery break down today? Map the friction points before touching the work.
02
Map
Lay out the full operational topology. Stakeholders, decision rights, dependencies.
03
Standardize
Define what "done" looks like for each output. No tribal knowledge.
04
Build
Construct the system — design systems, brief templates, asset libraries, AI pipelines.
05
Govern
Set decision gates. Who approves what, by when. Removes "waiting on email" days.
06
Ship
Output goes live on cadence. Weekly, monthly, or per-launch — whatever rhythm fits.
07
Measure
Track what matters — ops time saved, brief turnaround, brand consistency score.
08
Iterate
Loop closes here, then restarts. Continuous refinement, not one-off launches.
03

Your first 14 days.

Regardless of engagement model, the first two weeks follow this rhythm — stages 05 → 06 → 07 of the arc above. We front-load discovery so that by week three we are already shipping, not still aligning.

Stage 05 · 48h before kickoff — Pre-kickoff
Pre-kickoff brief + workspace invites
Two days before the kickoff call you receive: a personalized link to this onboarding doc, invites to Figma / Notion / shared drive, and 3 questions to think about. Answers welcome before the call, but if you are tight on time we cover them live.
Stage 06 · Day 1 — Kickoff call
90-minute kickoff, three outcomes
Strict tempo. We leave the call with three things: (1) decision map — who approves what, in what time; (2) three known blockers — things you already know will be hard; (3) delivery calendar — when to expect your first output. No "let's get to know each other" prologue.
Stage 07 · Day 2-10 — Discovery + Map
Async audit + 3 stakeholder interviews
We review your existing files (Figma, Drive, briefs, internal docs), run three 30-min interviews with key people on your side (e.g. COO + CMO + senior designer), and map your current process. Async-first — we do not block your team. You identify 3-5 friction points where time, quality, or decisions leak.
Stage 07 · Day 11-14 — Written diagnosis
One-page diagnosis + 60-day roadmap
End of week two: a one-page written diagnosis. What you said the problem was, what the real problem turned out to be (often not the same), where it specifically hurts, and a 60-day roadmap. This is a decision moment — if you agree, we move into Stage 08 build cadence the next week.
04

Communication cadence.

We work async-first. Our entire model depends on it — we do not bill for meetings, we bill for output. Here is the rhythm you can expect.

Weekly

  • Monday morning brief — written update covering what shipped last week, what is in flight, what needs your input. Read time: 5 minutes.
  • Thursday async check-in — short Loom video walking through deliverables in progress. Optional reply.
  • Friday delivery pack — every Friday by 5pm CET, the week's output is dropped into your shared workspace. Files, naming, variants, specs, handoff notes.

Monthly

  • 30-minute strategic sync — video call, first Tuesday of the month. Reviews KPIs, identifies blockers, sets focus for the next cycle.
  • Written outcome report — sent end of month. Hours saved, briefs delivered, decisions unblocked, brand consistency score.

Quarterly

  • Loop review — 90-min strategic session covering what is working, what to evolve, scope review for next quarter.
What we do not do

Daily standups. Slack noise. Status meetings. Email threads. If you find yourself typing "any update on…" — something is wrong with our system, not with us. Tell us.

07

What success looks like.

We measure ourselves by your operational metrics, not our hours billed. Here is the typical scorecard we set at kickoff, customized to your engagement. You sign off on the metric definition — that becomes the goal we report against.

  • Operations time reduced — hours saved per launch, per campaign, per opening
  • Brief-to-publish time — how long from a request to a finished asset
  • Brand consistency score — % of output that adheres to the design system without revision
  • Middle management hours freed — time your team gets back to focus on real decisions
  • Decision velocity — average time from request to approved direction
Reference

Past clients have seen 45% reduction in ops time per launch, 12 hours per week returned to middle management, and consistent brand quality across 300+ locations. Your numbers will be your own — but the directional impact is what we underwrite.

08

Questions other clients asked first.

What if my scope changes mid-engagement?
Scope changes happen — it is part of operating in real conditions. For Sprints and fixed-price engagements, we agree on the change in writing, add a short addendum, and adjust. For Retainers and Operating Partner engagements, scope evolution is built in.
Who is actually doing the work?
Reszek leads every engagement directly — strategy, design oversight, key decisions. For execution, we use a small network of trusted specialists when scope demands it. Every output passes through r352 quality review before reaching you. There is no junior-team handoff.
What if I am not happy with a deliverable?
Tell us immediately, in writing. We do one revision per deliverable within scope, no questions asked. If the issue is bigger — scope mismatch, direction divergence — we have a 24-hour reset protocol: we re-align in a 30-min call and adjust. We do not let unhappiness simmer.
How does pricing work?
Engagements are fixed-price (Sprints, Diagnostic, Enterprise Sprints) or monthly retainer (Retainer, Operating Partner). We never charge by the hour — it misaligns incentives. Pricing is set in your contract and only changes if scope materially changes, in writing, both sides sign.
Can my team learn the r3loop methodology?
Yes — and we encourage it. Operating Partner engagements include training your team to run r3loop independently. For other tiers, methodology documentation is available on request. We want you self-sufficient eventually. The system has to outlive our engagement.
What if I need to pause or cancel?
Sprints are non-refundable once started (work is in flight). Retainers and Operating Partner engagements have a 30-day notice period. Diagnostic has a 60-day money-back guarantee if recommendations are not actionable.
Do you sign NDAs?
Yes, standard practice. Your NDA or ours — whichever is faster. We have signed NDAs for every confidential engagement to date, including major enterprise clients.
Will you reference us in case studies?
Only with explicit written consent. Some clients prefer to remain confidential — we respect that without question. Others enjoy being featured as proof of operational excellence — we celebrate that too. You decide.

Operator language — quick glossary.

We use specific words for specific things. Here is a short translator so we are speaking the same language from day one.

r3loop
Our 8-step operating methodology. The framework every engagement runs through.
Design ops
The operational layer behind design output — briefs, standards, decisions, handoffs. Not the design itself.
Steady cadence
Predictable, weekly rhythm of output. The opposite of heroic launches followed by silence.
Multi-X
Operational complexity that comes from multiple locations, brands, products, or stakeholders. Our specialty.
Decision gate
A defined point where a specific person approves a specific decision. Removes "waiting on email" days.
Brief volume
How many design or campaign requests your team handles per period. Often the hidden bottleneck.
Asset library
Versioned, named, tagged collection of all brand assets — ready to deploy across channels without remaking.
AI-native
Production workflows that have AI baked in — automation handles 30-40% of repeatable work, humans handle decisions.
Handover
The moment we hand a system to your team to run independently. Includes docs, training, and a transition period.
Output
Tangible, named, deliverable artifact. The thing your team can use without us. Not a slide.
